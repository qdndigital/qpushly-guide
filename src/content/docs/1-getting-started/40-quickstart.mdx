This walks the shortest path to something working. It assumes you have
[installed the app](/docs/getting-started/install) and
[enabled the theme embed](/docs/getting-started/theme-embed).

## 1 · Set your notification defaults · 2 min

**Qpushly → Settings**.

Fill in three fields:

- **Store display name** — the name shoppers see as the sender.
- **Contact email** — where push services reach you about delivery problems.
- **Default notification icon URL** — the small square image on every
  notification. Use a square logo, at least 192×192px. Without one, browsers show
  a generic bell.

Save. While you are here, check the **Web Push** card shows a key — that means
your store's signing keypair has been generated. It is created automatically; you
never handle it.

## 2 · Become your own first subscriber · 1 min

Open your storefront in a normal browser window and accept the notification
prompt. Then check **Qpushly → Subscribers** — you should be there.

<Callout type="tip">
Do this before anything else. Every step below needs at least one subscriber to
send to, and testing on yourself is much faster than waiting for real traffic.
</Callout>

## 3 · Switch on welcome notifications · 3 min

**Qpushly → Automations → Welcome Notifications → Create Reminder**.

- **Notification Title** — short. Around 40 characters is what most browsers show
  before truncating.
- **Message** — one or two lines. Say what they just signed up for and what they
  get.
- **Delay Time** — leave it low for the first reminder.
- **Button text / link** — optional, but a button raises the click rate. Point it
  at a collection, not your homepage.
- **Status** — set it to active.

Save. New subscribers now get this automatically.

**Example that works:**

> **Title:** You're on the list
> **Message:** We'll ping you when new pieces drop and when sold-out sizes come
> back. First order? Use WELCOME10.
> **Button:** Shop new in → `/collections/new`

## 4 · Switch on abandoned cart recovery · 3 min

**Qpushly → Automations → Abandoned Cart Recovery**.

Create one reminder to start with. A cart counts as abandoned after **ten
minutes** of inactivity, and your delay is measured from that point.

Then add a second reminder at a longer delay if the first does not land. Three is
the maximum.

Do not write all three now. Get one working, see what it earns, then extend.

## 5 · Send a campaign · 2 min

**Qpushly → Campaigns → create a campaign**.

- **Campaign title** — internal name; also used as the notification title.
- **Description** — the notification body.
- **Campaign URL** — where a click lands. Use a real collection or product URL.
- **Image** — optional. A wide product shot works better than a logo here.
- **Target audience** and **Campaign type** — leave the defaults for a first test.
- **Publish time** — send now.

You should get the notification within about a minute.

<Callout type="warning" title="Scheduled sends are not instant to the second">
Qpushly processes scheduled work on a sweep that runs every ten minutes, so a
campaign scheduled for 09:00 goes out between 09:00 and 09:10. Fine for a drop
announcement; do not build a countdown around it.
</Callout>

## 6 · Check the numbers · 1 min

Back on the **Dashboard** you now have four tiles — revenue generated,
subscribers, campaigns sent, impressions — plus a subscriber-growth chart you can
switch between 7, 15, 30 and 60 days.

The campaign you just sent shows its own impressions and clicks under
**Campaigns**. Revenue only appears once a notification click leads to an actual
order — see [Campaign results](/docs/campaigns/campaign-results) for how that is
matched.

## What to do in week one

1. Add a **`data-qpushly-subscribe` button** to your sold-out product pages
   ([how](/docs/getting-started/theme-embed)). This is usually the single biggest
   change to subscriber growth.
2. Switch on **back in stock** — it needs no copywriting effort and it sends to
   people who have already told you they want the product.
3. Leave **browse abandonment** and **price drop** until you have enough
   subscribers for the numbers to mean anything.
