The Dashboard is the first screen in Qpushly. It has four metric tiles, a
subscriber-growth chart, and a card for your most recent campaign.

## The four tiles

**Revenue generated** — the total value of orders Qpushly could attribute to a
notification. See [Campaign results](/docs/campaigns/campaign-results) for exactly
how an order gets attributed; the short version is that a notification click tags
the cart, and the order carries the tag.

**Subscribers** — how many push subscriptions your store holds. Remember this
counts *browsers*, so one shopper on a laptop and a phone is two.

**Total campaigns sent** — campaigns that have been published, not automations.

**Impressions consumed** — how many notifications were delivered.

<Callout type="note" title="Delivered is not seen">
An impression means the notification reached the browser. It does not mean the
shopper looked at it. A notification that appeared while the laptop lid was shut
still counts. Treat impressions as your reach, and clicks as your only real
engagement signal.
</Callout>

## The growth chart

**Subscriber gain** plots new subscribers per day. The dropdown switches between
**7, 15, 30 and 60 days**.

What to look for:

- **A flat line at zero** after you enabled the embed usually means the embed is
  not on your *live* theme, or the prompt is not being reached. Go back to
  [Turn on the theme app embed](/docs/getting-started/theme-embed).
- **A step change** normally traces to a traffic source or to adding a
  `data-qpushly-subscribe` button somewhere new.
- **A steady drift down in daily additions** with steady traffic is normal — the
  visitors most willing to subscribe do so first.

## Latest campaign

Shows the most recent campaign with its own counters, so you can check on a send
without going to the Campaigns list.

## What the dashboard cannot tell you

Being straight about the gaps saves you drawing the wrong conclusion:

- **No unsubscribe count.** When a shopper blocks notifications in their browser,
  the browser does not tell your store. The subscription simply stops being
  deliverable. So subscriber count can overstate your reachable audience.
- **No per-day revenue breakdown.** Revenue is a running total for the range, not
  a series you can plot against sends.
- **Impressions are not deduplicated per shopper.** Two devices belonging to the
  same person are two impressions.
- **Automations are not broken out here.** For per-automation clicks, impressions,
  revenue and conversion rate, go to
  [Automations](/docs/automations/automations-overview) — the list shows all five
  with their own numbers.

## The free-plan banner

link to Shopify's plan page. See
