## Install

1. Open the Qpushly listing on the **Shopify App Store**.
2. Click **Add app**, then choose the store you want to install it on.
3. Review the permissions (see below) and click **Install**.
4. Shopify drops you into Qpushly's **Dashboard**.

That is it — no API keys, no scripts to paste, no account to create. Qpushly
appears in your Shopify admin sidebar under **Apps**.

## What the permissions are for

Shopify shows you a list of what the app can access. Qpushly asks for four
things, and only four:

| Permission | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| **View products** | To receive Shopify's product-update notifications, which is what triggers back-in-stock and price-drop alerts. Without it those two automations can never fire. |
| **View orders** | To match an order back to the notification that was clicked, so campaigns and automations can show orders and revenue. |
| **View and manage files** | To store the images you upload for campaigns and your notification logo in your own Shopify Files. |

<Callout type="note" title="What it does not ask for">
Qpushly does not ask to *edit* your products, orders or inventory, and does not
ask for customer records. If a future version needs more, Shopify will ask you to
approve the change — an app cannot quietly widen its own access.
</Callout>

## The next step is not optional

Installing the app gives you the admin screens. It does **not** put anything on
your storefront yet, which means no visitor can subscribe and every screen in the
app will be empty.

<Callout type="danger" title="Do this next">
Go to **[Turn on the theme app embed](/docs/getting-started/theme-embed)**. Until
you do, Qpushly cannot collect a single subscriber. This is the number one reason
a new install looks broken.
</Callout>

## If the install fails

**"This app can't be installed"** — your store may be on a plan that blocks
custom or embedded apps, or a store owner permission is missing. Ask whoever owns
the store to install it, or to grant you the **Manage and install apps**
permission under Settings → Users.

**You installed it but cannot find it** — Shopify admin → **Apps** → Qpushly.
Pin it there and it will stay in your sidebar.

**A blank screen after install** — reload the page once. If it persists, open the
app in a normal browser window rather than a private/incognito one; embedded apps
need cookies from `admin.shopify.com`.
