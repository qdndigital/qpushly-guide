**Qpushly → Settings.** Five cards.

## General

**Store display name** — the name shoppers associate with the notification. Use the
name they know you by, not your legal entity.

**Contact email** — embedded in the signed request Qpushly sends to push services
(Google, Mozilla, Apple). Those services use it to reach you if your store starts
producing delivery problems. Set a real, monitored address.

<Callout type="warning" title="Set the contact email">
If you leave this blank, a generic fallback is used. It is not a hard failure, but it
means the one channel a push service has for warning you about a delivery problem
goes nowhere.
</Callout>

## Notification defaults

**Default notification icon URL** — the small square image on every notification.
Square, at least 192×192px, and it should read clearly at about 48px because that is
how big it usually appears. Without one, browsers show a generic bell.

**Default click URL** — where a notification goes when it has no link of its own. Set
it to a collection you are happy to land people on.

## Branding

**Logo upload** — uploaded to your own Shopify Files and served from Shopify's CDN.
Under 5MB.

## Web Push

Read-only status. It shows whether your store's signing keypair has been generated,
and the public half of it.

You never need to touch this. The keypair is created automatically the first time
your store needs it, and it proves to browsers that a notification genuinely came
from your store.

<Callout type="note" title="What the keypair means for you">
It is generated **per store** and stored against your store's session. If that session
is ever wiped — for instance if you uninstall and reinstall — a new keypair is
generated, and push subscriptions created under the old key stop being deliverable.
The storefront detects this on the shopper's next visit and re-subscribes them
automatically, but subscribers who never return again are lost.

The practical advice: **avoid uninstall/reinstall cycles** on a store with a
subscriber list you care about.
</Callout>
