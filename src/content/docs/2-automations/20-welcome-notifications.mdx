**Fires when:** a visitor subscribes to notifications.

This is the highest-odds automation you have. The shopper just chose to hear from
you, they are on your site, and they are already interested. Nothing else you send
gets that.

## Set it up

**Automations → Welcome Notifications → Create Reminder.**

- **Delay Time** — keep the first reminder short. The value of this message decays
  fast; by tomorrow the moment has gone.
- **Title** — confirm what they did in a way that sounds like a benefit, not a
  receipt.
- **Message** — say what you will actually send them and how often. Managing the
  expectation now is what stops them blocking you in a month.
- **Button** — point at a collection, not the homepage.

## What to write

Do:

- Confirm the subscription in the title.
- Name what they will get: restocks, drops, price cuts.
- Give a reason to click *now* — a collection, a new arrival, a first-order code.

Don't:

- Open with "Thanks for subscribing!" and nothing else. That is a receipt, and
  receipts do not get clicked.
- Promise a frequency you will not keep.
- Send three welcome messages. One is right; two is the most anyone needs.

**Example:**

> **Title:** You're on the list
> **Message:** We'll ping you when new pieces land and when sold-out sizes come
> back — a couple of times a month, not more.
> **Button:** See what's new → `/collections/new`

<Callout type="tip" title="Use it to set frequency expectations">
The subscribers who block you later are usually the ones who did not know what
they signed up for. One honest sentence here — "a couple of times a month" — is
the cheapest retention work available to you.
</Callout>

## A second reminder

If you add one, make it do a different job. A useful pattern is: reminder 1
confirms and points at new arrivals; reminder 2, a day or two later, introduces
the thing that makes your store worth following — the restock alerts, the
made-to-order process, whatever it is.

Do not use reminder 2 to repeat the discount code from reminder 1.

## Checking it works

Subscribe from your own storefront in a browser you have not subscribed in before,
then wait for your delay plus up to ten minutes. Your run shows up under the
Welcome Notifications card's stats.

<Callout type="note">
An existing subscriber will not get a welcome message again. If you want to
re-test, reset notification permission for the site in your browser and clear the
site's local storage, then subscribe again — that creates a new subscriber.
</Callout>
