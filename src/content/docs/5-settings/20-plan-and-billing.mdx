## Billed by Shopify

Qpushly uses **Shopify Managed Pricing**. Every part of the money side is handled by
Shopify:

- The plan list and prices are hosted by Shopify.
- Approving a plan happens in your Shopify admin.
- The charge appears on your normal **Shopify invoice**, with your other apps.
- Upgrading, downgrading and cancelling are all done on Shopify's plan page.

Qpushly never sees or stores a card, and you never need to contact support to change
a plan.

<Callout type="note" title="Prices are whatever Shopify shows you">
Because plans live in Shopify rather than in the app, Shopify's plan page and the App
Store listing are the authority on what a plan costs and includes. If any page —
including this guide — disagrees with what Shopify shows at checkout, Shopify is
right.
</Callout>

## Where to find it

Two routes, both landing on Shopify's own page:

- **Dashboard** → the plan banner, if you are on the free plan.
- **Settings** → **Plan & billing** → **View plans & upgrade** / **Manage plan**.

## Changing or cancelling

On Shopify's plan page: pick a different plan, or cancel. Cancellation takes effect at
the end of the current billing period — you keep access until then.

Uninstalling the app also stops future charges.

## What happens when you downgrade

**Nothing is deleted and nothing stops sending.** Your subscriber list, campaigns
and automation copy stay exactly as they are, and any automation you already set up
keeps running.

What changes is what you can *add*: on the free plan you cannot create a new gated
automation or add a second reminder. You can still edit the ones you have.

<Callout type="tip">
That is deliberate. A plan limit that silently switched off notifications a
merchant had come to rely on would be worse than no limit at all.
</Callout>

## What happens when you uninstall

1. Your store's access credentials are removed **immediately**.
2. Shopify stops the recurring charge.
3. Shopify then sends a shop-redaction request, and **every record for your store is
   deleted** — subscribers, campaigns, automations, logs, settings.

<Callout type="danger" title="Export your subscribers before uninstalling">
The subscriber list is the one thing you cannot rebuild. Go to
**[Import and export](/docs/subscribers/import-export)** and download the CSV before
you uninstall — after the redaction it is gone, and support cannot recover it.
</Callout>

## Trials

A paid plan carries Shopify's standard trial before the first charge; the exact
length is shown on Shopify's plan page when you subscribe.

The **free plan is not a trial**. It does not expire and it does not turn into a paid
plan on its own.

## Billing questions we cannot answer

Because Shopify is the merchant of record for app subscriptions, anything about
invoices, tax, refunds or payment methods goes to **Shopify support**, not to us. We
can help with what a plan *includes*; Shopify handles what you were *charged*.
