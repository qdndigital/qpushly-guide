## Setup

**I installed the app and nothing happens.**
You still need to enable the theme app embed. This is the single most common issue —
[here is how](/docs/getting-started/theme-embed).

**Why does Qpushly show its own invite instead of the browser's prompt?**
Because a browser only lets you ask once, ever, and a shopper who blocks
notifications can never be asked again. Asking with our own invite first means the
browser prompt is only spent on people who already said yes — and Safari refuses a
prompt that did not come from a click anyway. You can turn the invite off and ask
only from your own button. [Settings](/docs/getting-started/theme-embed).

**Do I need a developer?**
No. Installing and enabling the embed are both point-and-click. The only optional code
is a one-line attribute if you want a "Notify me" button in a specific place.

**Will it slow my storefront down?**
The embed adds three small deferred scripts. They do not block rendering, and there are
no third-party fonts, images or trackers.

**Can I use it alongside another push app?**
You should not. Two push apps on one storefront produce duplicate notifications and
untrustworthy numbers in both. Remove the old one —
[details](/docs/subscribers/import-export).

## Subscribers

**Do shoppers give an email address?**
No. That is the point of push. Email addresses appear on a subscriber only where your
own Shopify order data supplies one.

**Why is my subscriber count higher than my customer count?**
A subscriber is a browser, not a person — laptop plus phone is two. See
[The subscriber list](/docs/subscribers/subscriber-list).

**Can I see who unsubscribed?**
No, and no push tool can. When a shopper blocks notifications, their browser does not
tell your store. Those subscribers stay in your list but stop being deliverable.

**Can I import my email list?**
No. A push subscription can only be created by the shopper's own browser. Email
contacts have to opt in from your storefront.

**Can I import from another push provider?**
Yes, via CSV — but expect some endpoints to be dead already.
[How](/docs/subscribers/import-export).

## Sending

**Why did my scheduled campaign go out a few minutes late?**
Scheduled work runs on a sweep every ten minutes. Schedule fifteen minutes early if the
exact moment matters. [More](/docs/campaigns/schedule-and-audience).

**How often should I send?**
Two to four campaigns a month for most stores, plus whatever the automations send. Push
is more intrusive than email and the penalty for over-sending is permanent — a blocked
shopper cannot be won back from inside the app.

**Can I set quiet hours?**
There is no quiet-hours setting. Control it by choosing when you schedule, and check
your subscribers' timezone spread in the subscriber list.

**How many reminders can an automation have?**
Three. The limit is deliberate.
[Why](/docs/automations/automations-overview).

**Can I segment by purchase history or spend?**
No. Segmentation is by browser, OS and device — attributes of the subscription, not the
customer.

## Numbers

**Does "impressions" mean shoppers saw it?**
No — delivered to the browser. Clicks are your only real engagement signal.

**Why is attributed revenue lower than I expect?**
Every gap in attribution pushes the number down: the click tag expires after about 30
minutes, lives on one device, and can be dropped by a theme that rebuilds the cart.
Treat it as a floor. [Full explanation](/docs/campaigns/campaign-results).

**Qpushly and my email tool both claim the same order.**
Both are right by their own model. Do not add them together.

## Devices

**Does it work on iPhone?**
Only if the shopper has added your store to their home screen — Apple's rule, not ours.
Most iPhone traffic will never see the prompt.

**Which browsers work?**
Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Opera and Samsung Internet on desktop and Android; Safari on
macOS. [Details](/docs/getting-started/welcome).

## Billing

**How am I charged?**
Through Shopify, on your normal Shopify invoice. Qpushly never handles a card.
[More](/docs/settings/plan-and-billing).

**Can I cancel myself?**
Yes, from Shopify's plan page. No need to contact support.

**What happens to my data if I uninstall?**
Credentials go immediately; everything else is deleted when Shopify sends the
shop-redaction request.

**A shopper asked me for their data — what do I do?**
Open **Apps → Qpushly → Data requests**, download the JSON and send it on. Qpushly
assembles it for you when Shopify forwards the request.
[Walkthrough](/docs/help/data-requests).

## Something else

Work through [Troubleshooting](/docs/help/troubleshooting) first — it is ordered by how
often each cause turns out to be the real one. If you still need us, include your
`myshopify.com` domain and whether the theme embed is enabled on your live theme.
